# Activity 3 — Accessibility Challenge
App Chosen:  *Instagram*

I explored how Instagram can be redesigned to improve accessibility for:
- Users who are colorblind
- Users who can’t hear
- Users with shaky hands

## ✅ My Accessibility Redesign Ideas
| Accessibility Need | Current Issue | Redesign Solution |
|------------------|---------------|------------------|
| **Colorblind users** | Status indicators (read/unread messages, story rings) rely heavily on color differences | Use clearer patterns, text labels, or shape changes for state differences |
| **Cannot hear** | Reels/Stories often rely on audio cues or dialogue | Auto-generated captions by default & better sound-to-visual cues (vibration, animations synced to audio beats) |
| **Shaky hands** | Small tap targets for like, comment, and story navigation | Larger interactive areas, gesture alternatives (with voice), reduced accidental taps with more spacing |
