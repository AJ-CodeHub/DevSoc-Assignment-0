# Activity 2 — Spot Bad Design

I saw the examples of poorly designed UI and here is what I think makes them ineffective.

## ❌ Why were these designs bad?

- Cluttered layouts make it hard for users to focus on what’s important
- Inconsistent spacing, fonts, and shapes create visual confusion
- Actions are unclear — users can’t tell what a button will do
- Too many competing colors reduce readability and cause visual fatigue

## ✅ How I would fix them

- Simplify the layout and group related elements together
- Use visual cues to guide the user rather than text everywhere
- Maintain consistency in colors, typography, and button styles
- Increase contrast so text is readable for everyone
