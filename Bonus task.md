# 💡 Bonus Task – Solving a Daily Annoyance

## 😩 Problem
Students often **lose small items** (bottles, ID cards, umbrellas, chargers, etc.) around campus — and finding them back is pure luck.  
There’s no central system to report or check lost items, and asking around or posting on random WhatsApp groups is chaotic.

---

## Design Idea: “FoundIt” App Screen

**Goal:**  
A simple campus app screen where students can **report lost/found items**, upload a picture, and get notified if a match is found.

---

## 📱 Screen Description

**Title:** FoundIt 🔍  

**Top Section:**  
- Tabs: `Lost Items | Found Items`  
- Search bar: “Search item name or location”  

**Main Feed:**  
- Each card shows:  
  - 📸 Item photo  
  - Title: “Black Bottle with Red Cap”  
  - Location: “Found near LTC”  
  - Time: “2 hours ago”  
  - Button: “Contact Finder”  

**Bottom Buttons:**  
- ➕ “Report Lost Item”  
- 📤 “Report Found Item”

---

## Why it works
- Centralizes all lost & found info, no more group spam.  
- Visual search (photos) helps match items faster.  
- No need to write long emails to SWD, the process becomes instant and self-service
- Saves time for both the person who lost and the one who found the item.
